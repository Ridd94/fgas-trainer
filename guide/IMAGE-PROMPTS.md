# Illustration prompts for F-Gas Category I in Pictures

One image per topic page. Save each as `guide/images/<slug>.png` (or .webp/.jpg). Run `node guide/build.js` and any page with an image uses it in place of its schematic drawing; pages without one keep the drawing. Aim for 1600 x 900 px or larger, landscape.

## Style, prepend to every prompt

> Flat vector educational infographic illustration, clean thick dark outlines, soft pastel palette (light blue #d6ecf7 for cold and low pressure, light orange #fde3cf for hot and high pressure, yellow #fff1c2 and mint #d8f1e3 as accents), white background, friendly technical-manual look in the style of a modern training poster. Landscape 16:9. NO text, NO letters, NO numbers, NO labels anywhere in the image. Realistic proportions of real HVAC equipment.

## Checklist for whoever generates them

- No text, letters or numbers in any image; the page carries the words.
- Cold side blue, hot side orange, everywhere, so the colour code stays consistent across pages.
- UK recovery cylinders are grey with a yellow shoulder. Nitrogen cylinders are dark grey. Manifold: blue left, red right, yellow centre hose.
- Reject any image that shows a safety violation as if it were correct (flame near a cylinder, braze on a charged line) unless the prompt asks for it with a red cross overlay.
- After dropping images in, rebuild and skim the PDF for any page where the image is cropped or too small.
